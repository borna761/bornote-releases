# borNote privacy policy

*Last updated: 9 November 2026*

borNote collects nothing. It has no accounts, no analytics, no tracking and no servers of its own, and it doesn't send your notes or any data about you to the developer or to anyone else.

## Your notes

Your notes are plain Markdown files in a folder you choose on your own device. borNote reads and writes that folder and nothing else. If you keep the folder in iCloud Drive, OneDrive, Google Drive or Dropbox, that provider's own app syncs it under that provider's terms. borNote isn't involved in that sync.

## Your calendar

If you allow it, borNote reads your Mac's calendar events so you can take notes on a meeting. The events are read on your Mac and never leave it. borNote only reads your calendar and never changes it.

## Updates

The version of borNote downloaded from GitHub contacts GitHub to check whether a newer version is available. That request goes to GitHub and is subject to [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). The Mac App Store version doesn't do this. The App Store updates it.

## Links

Links in borNote, such as the Buy Me a Coffee link and links inside your notes, open in your default web browser. borNote doesn't send anything to those sites. What happens after that is governed by the site you open.

## Children

borNote doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated date.

## Contact
